# HR Policy Document — AI-Hunt

## Leave Policy
Employees get 21 total leave days per year: 12 sick leaves and 9 casual/earned leaves. Leave must be applied for via the company employee portal, and the employee must inform their manager. Employees taking extra leave or unapproved leave must provide a valid reason later.

## Work From Home (WFH) Policy
Work From Home is allowed only for a serious reason, health issue, or family reasons — it is not a standard/default option. Employees must contact their project manager and HR, and update their status from "Work From Office" to "Work From Home" in the system. Employees must still work within standard work hours while on WFH.

## Insurance Policy
Health insurance is signed up for during the joining process. If an employee leaves the company, only the premium amount paid is refunded — the insurance coverage itself ends immediately and does not carry any interest or additional returns.

## Travel & Reimbursement Policy
There is no travel or reimbursement policy for personal use. All costs are covered if travel or money is incurred specifically for company purposes.

## Notice Period & Resignation
Employees must serve at least 1 month of notice period before resignation is finalized. Resignation is only accepted after the employee has completed at least 6 months of employment. To resign, employees must apply through the company portal and also send a written email to HR.
